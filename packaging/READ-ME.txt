utilBox

One mailbox icon sits at the top right of the screen, next to Control Center.

Click the mailbox to open the clipboard, with the scratchpad directly under it.
Click the mailbox again to hide both. The scratchpad stays on top of other apps,
browsers, fullscreen windows, and Spaces until you hide it. Shrink it to a
Scratchpad pill and drag that anywhere. Notes stay on this Mac until you delete them.

Drag a file, some text, or an image onto the mailbox and the clipboard opens
so you can drop it in. Move the drag away and the clipboard closes.
Hovering the mailbox with nothing on the cursor does not open it.

Click an item in the clipboard, then press Command-V to paste it.
Click outside the clipboard, or press Esc, to close it and leave the scratchpad open.
Drop text, images, or files onto the open clipboard to keep them there.
Pin an item to hold onto it. The clipboard keeps 100 recent copies and 50 pins.

Install
1. Drag utilBox into the Applications folder.
2. The first time you open it, macOS says the developer cannot be verified.
   Click Done, then right-click utilBox, choose Open, then Open.
   On newer macOS: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway.
   That question is asked once. Later launches open normally.
3. Or double-click "Install utilBox.command". It copies the app into
   Applications and clears the download quarantine flag. macOS may ask once
   about that installer. The app itself is unsigned, so Apple cannot skip
   every warning without a paid developer account.

Quit and Open at Login are under the ··· button, or right-click the mailbox.
Clipboard history and notes stay on this Mac, in your Library folder. Nothing is uploaded.
